THE JOURNEY

From first quote to completion — organised.

The first build is deliberately simple: one client journey, one matter record and one clear view of what is happening.

1Tell us about the move

Sale, purchase, remortgage or both. Property value, tenure, mortgage, chain and timing.

2Compare providers

Clear legal fee, VAT, expected disbursements and service information.

3Instruct once

Threshold builds the instruction pack and sends the agreed information to the selected provider.

4Upload & verify

Documents, ID workflow, source-of-funds information and requested forms in one place.

5Track the matter

IRIS converts updates into plain-English stages, actions, waits and blockers.

6Complete

Final actions, completion confirmation and a clean record of the journey.
