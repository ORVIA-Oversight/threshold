WHAT WE DO

Untangle without taking sides.

We bring dates, documents, versions and key events together. We distinguish what is recorded, what is supported, what conflicts, and what is simply not known.

  • Chronology of key events
  • Cross-reference reports, emails, assessments and notes
  • Identify gaps and contradictions
  • Follow who knew what and when
  • Produce clearer material you can use with professionals
Missing information is not proof of wrongdoing.

It is missing information. Threshold shows what is present, what is absent and what cannot reasonably be concluded from the record.
