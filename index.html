THE THRESHOLD PRINCIPLE

A clear process before an important move.

We don't make decisions for you. We make sure you have the fullest picture we can reasonably assemble before you decide.

🔊LISTEN

What are you trying to decide?

🔍CHECK

What information is available?

🔗CONNECT

What does it tell us together?

❓QUESTION

What's missing, conflicting or uncertain?

✅PREPARE

What should you know before you move?

→ACT

You make the final decision.

We never say BUY or DO NOT BUY.

We say: here's the picture. Here's what appears reassuring, what needs checking, and what questions remain. Then you decide.
