WHAT HAPPENS

Your evidence, laid out clearly.

  • Your story — what you remember versus what the records support
  • Your timeline — what happened and when
  • Your records — what the documents actually show
  • Other accounts — where they agree, differ or remain uncertain
  • The unknowns — what cannot presently be established
  • Preparation — practise explaining your own evidence calmly and honestly
STRICT BOUNDARY

What we never do

  • Tell you what to say
  • Invent or alter evidence
  • Rehearse preferred answers
  • Hide inconvenient information
We help you understand YOUR evidence. We do not give you a script.
