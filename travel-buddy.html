WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT

Some trips need more than flights and a hotel.

  • Research relevant hospitals or specialist centres and practical distance from the proposed accommodation
  • Organise local emergency numbers and available emergency-care information
  • Check climate, heat, UV and seasonal practical factors
  • Surface transport, access, safety and essential local information
  • Identify official sources for passport, visa, entry and health-document requirements
  • Check accommodation against mobility, rest, refrigeration and support needs

MEDICATION & MEDICAL EQUIPMENT

Plan for the things that can go wrong away from home.

A sensor can fail after swimming. A pump can be damaged. Medication can be delayed with luggage. Refrigerated medicines need the right storage. Travel Ready helps turn those possibilities into questions and contingency checks before departure.

  • Medication list using the information supplied by the traveller and their clinical team
  • Questions about extra supplies for delays or disruption
  • Backup planning for CGM sensors, pumps, inhalers and other essential devices
  • Storage and temperature requirements to confirm with the appropriate clinician, pharmacist, airline and accommodation provider
  • Medical equipment and additional baggage questions for the airline
  • Doctor-letter, prescription and supporting-document prompts where appropriate
  • Emergency contacts and insurer assistance details organised together
ORVIA does not prescribe or decide medication quantities. We organise the questions, requirements and contingencies; the traveller's clinician or pharmacist remains responsible for clinical advice and prescriptions.

HOLIDAY HEALTH PASSPORT

One clear pack if somebody needs to understand your needs quickly.

Travel Ready can organise the traveller's supplied information into a practical Holiday Health Passport — more detailed than a basic hospital passport, but still clear enough to use under pressure.

  • Identity and emergency contacts
  • Conditions and allergies
  • Medication and schedules
  • Medical devices and backup equipment
  • Communication and accessibility needs
  • Clinical-team details
  • Insurance assistance information
  • Airline assistance information
  • Accommodation and destination details
  • Relevant emergency-care information
  • Traveller's consent preferences
  • Key documents checklist

With explicit consent, ORVIA can also prepare information for the traveller to share with airlines, accommodation providers or healthcare teams. Any live communication or disclosure would follow the traveller's agreed consent and scope.

HONEST BUDGETING

The real trip cost — not just the headline rate.

  • Separate rooms priced correctly where sharing is not suitable
  • Baggage, transfers, taxis, meals, tickets and contingencies shown separately where information is available
  • Specialist insurance kept visible rather than buried inside the holiday price
  • Your own affordability limit set before booking so the full trip cost can be tested against it

IMPORTANT CLARIFICATION

Planning and research — not medical advice or travel sales.

Travel Ready is an information-organisation, planning and research service.

  • ORVIA does not provide medical advice
  • ORVIA does not prescribe medication
  • ORVIA does not sell insurance
  • ORVIA does not book travel
  • Clinical decisions remain with the traveller and their clinical team
  • Insurance decisions remain with the insurer
  • Bookings and final live checks remain with the traveller or their trusted representative
CLEAR BOUNDARY

Travel Ready helps organise the right questions and information so you can make better-informed decisions with the people responsible for clinical, insurance and travel advice.
