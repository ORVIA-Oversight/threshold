ORVIA TRAVEL READY

Travel planned properly,
around you.

Everything organised. Every practical risk considered. Better preparation before you go.

When health, mobility, independence or changing needs matter, Travel Ready brings destination research, accommodation, clinical questions, insurance checks, transport, costs and emergency information into one clear planning picture.

WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT

Some trips need more than flights and a hotel.

  • Research relevant hospitals or specialist centres and practical distance from the proposed accommodation
  • Organise local emergency numbers and available emergency-care information
  • Check climate, heat, UV and seasonal practical factors
  • Surface transport, access, safety and essential local information
  • Identify the official sources for passport, visa, entry and health-document requirements
BUILT AROUND THE PERSON

Planning that reflects real needs.

Separate rooms, accessibility, air conditioning, lift access, overnight support, cancellation terms and proximity to essentials can all matter.

HEALTH & SAFETY FRAMEWORK

Questions prepared before commitments are made.

Clinical clearance

Questions to take to the traveller's consultant, GP or specialist team where health makes this necessary.

Insurance specification

Cover points, disclosures, repatriation and emergency questions to confirm directly with an appropriately authorised insurer or broker.

Medication & documents

A practical checklist for medicines, supporting letters and documentation required by clinicians, carriers or destination authorities.

Airport assistance

What support may be useful and what needs to be requested with the airline or airport.

HONEST BUDGETING

The real trip cost — not just the headline rate.

  • Separate rooms priced correctly where sharing is not suitable
  • Baggage, transfers, taxis, meals, tickets and contingencies shown separately where information is available
  • Specialist insurance kept visible rather than buried inside the holiday price
  • Your own affordability limit set before booking so the full trip cost can be tested against it

SIX CLEAR GATES

Before anything is booked, these six questions should be closed.

1Clinical position

The proposed trip has been discussed with the appropriate clinical team where needed.

2Insurance position

Relevant conditions have been disclosed and required cover confirmed in writing.

3Accommodation

Room setup, access, visitor rules and other non-negotiables checked.

4Airport assistance

Any required assistance requested and documented.

5Baggage & transfers

Hold baggage, medication carriage and transfer arrangements checked.

6Medication & paperwork

Required medicines, supporting documents and emergency information prepared.

WHAT THIS DEPTH LOOKS LIKE

More than a brochure.

In a recent Travel Ready-style planning exercise, the work included destination healthcare, seasonal heat and UV, local safety, event timing, transport, realistic ground costs, separate-room requirements, live package comparisons, a ranked shortlist, clinical questions, insurance questions and an emergency-information pack. Destination facts, prices and availability are time-sensitive and must always be rechecked before booking.

IMPORTANT CLARIFICATION

Planning and research — not medical advice or travel sales.

Travel Ready is an information-organisation, planning and research service.

  • ORVIA does not provide medical advice
  • ORVIA does not sell insurance
  • ORVIA does not book travel
  • Clinical decisions remain with the traveller and their clinical team
  • Insurance decisions remain with the insurer
  • Bookings and final live checks remain with the traveller or their trusted representative
CLEAR BOUNDARY

Use the right professional for the decision.

Travel Ready helps organise the right questions and information so you can make better-informed decisions with the people responsible for clinical, insurance and travel advice.
